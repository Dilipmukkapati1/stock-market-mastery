# Chapter 9 — Capstone: A Pre-IPO Decision Framework

**Learning objective:** Assemble everything in this book into a reusable checklist for evaluating any pre-IPO opportunity. This is a thinking tool, not a recommendation — it will not tell you to buy or avoid Glean.

## The eight-question framework

Answer each in writing, with a bucket label (1/2/3) on every number you use:

1. **What do they keep?** State net recurring revenue. For Glean: ~$300M ARR, seat-based SaaS — no pass-through adjustment needed. If you can't compute this, stop.
2. **Who pays?** Name the customers and estimate concentration. For Glean: ~200 customers (Intuit, Booking.com, Zillow named); concentration and churn undisclosed.
3. **Why can't customers leave?** State the moat in one falsifiable sentence. For Glean: "Permissions-aware cross-app search that hyperscalers structurally can't replicate for each other's suites." Falsifier: a Copilot/Gemini release with true cross-suite permissions.
4. **What breaks the thesis?** Name the single event that halves the investment. For Glean: growth deceleration into multiple compression (no scandal required), or a permissions incident.
5. **What am I actually buying?** Preferred or common? Last 409A? Transfer restrictions? For Glean: no public 409A; NPM's $44.15 is a model estimate, not a price — any offer needs independent verification.
6. **What does the price imply?** Compute the multiple on ARR and state what growth it demands. ~24x at $7.2B demands sustained 50%+ growth; the 72x at pricing has already been grown into once.
7. **What don't I know?** List the missing disclosures (Chapter 8, Technique 4). Size your position — hypothetically — against the unknowns, not the knowns.
8. **What is my exit if nothing happens?** No IPO timeline; exit paths are acquisition (two category takeouts in 2025 set precedent), tender offer, or secondary sale — price each path's plausibility.

## Red-flag ledger — Glean (observed, not alleged)

- ~24x revenue multiple with zero room for growth deceleration
- Permanent hyperscaler bundling threat (Copilot/Gemini)
- Permissions architecture is a single point of existential trust — unpriced risk
- No audited financials, no 409A, no retention/churn/margin disclosure
- Aging $7.2B mark (16 months) resting on reported ARR, not a negotiated price
- Vendor-adjacent benchmark claims drew methodological skepticism

## Green-flag ledger — Glean (observed, not alleged)

- $100M → $300M ARR in ~16 months of high-quality contracted SaaS revenue
- Reportedly cash-flow positive — dilution-free growth, raises were optional
- Two strategic takeouts in-category (Moveworks $2.85B, Sana $1.05B) validate and price the asset
- Neutrality across vendors' apps is structurally defensible against bundlers
- Decelerating valuation step-ups (2.2x → 2.1x → 1.6x) show compounding, not narrative repricing
