# Chapter 10 — Mastery Quiz

Answer each, then check. Target: ≥90%.

**1. Glean's last priced valuation and round are:**
A. $4.6B, Series E (Sep 2024)
B. $7.2B, Series F (Jun 2025)
C. $2.2B, Series D (2024)
*Answer: B. Series F, $150M at $7.2B led by Wellington. No 2026 round exists. (Ch. 4)*

**2. On $300M ARR, the $7.2B valuation implies a multiple of about:**
A. 72x
B. 24x
C. 8x
*Answer: B. $7.2B ÷ $300M ≈ 24x. 72x was the multiple on $100M ARR at pricing. (Ch. 4)*

**3. Nasdaq Private Market's "$44.15/share" for Glean is:**
A. The last trade price
B. A model estimate, not a transaction
C. The 409A valuation
*Answer: B. Explicitly a model estimate from market activity and public data. (Ch. 1)*

**4. Moveworks was acquired by ServiceNow for $2.85B at roughly what revenue multiple?**
A. ~5x
B. ~20x
C. ~2x
*Answer: B. ~$140M+ 2024 revenue → ~20x, including a control premium. (Ch. 5)*

**5. Coveo's ~2x public multiple matters because it shows:**
A. What Glean will IPO at
B. The compression floor if Glean's growth breaks
C. That enterprise search is worthless
*Answer: B. It's the mature, slower-growing comp — the downside anchor. (Ch. 2)*

**6. Which bucket does "reportedly cash-flow positive" belong in?**
A. Company-disclosed
B. Press-reported
C. Analyst-estimated
*Answer: B. It's an investor's quoted claim in press — credible but unaudited. (Ch. 8)*

**7. Glean's strongest moat candidate is:**
A. Its 40+ LLM routing
B. Permissions-aware architecture across 100+ connectors
C. Its 275+ connectors alone
*Answer: B. Permissions enforcement is hard to replicate correctly and existential to get right. (Ch. 6)*

**8. Why can Glean's valuation fall while its revenue grows?**
A. It can't — rising revenue always raises valuation
B. Multiple compression (e.g., 24x → 8x) can outrun revenue growth
C. Only fraud causes that
*Answer: B. $350M × 8x = $2.8B < $7.2B, with revenue still growing. (Ch. 7)*

**9. The "zombie number" caught in this book's research was:**
A. A fake $300M ARR figure
B. A low-quality aggregator's claim of a "$150M Series F in early 2026"
C. The $44.15 NPM estimate
*Answer: B. It contradicts all primary reporting; Series F closed June 2025. (Ch. 8)*

**10. Glean's best structural defense against Microsoft/Google bundling is:**
A. Lower prices
B. Neutrality — cross-vendor search the bundlers can't do for each other's suites
C. Exclusive LLM access
*Answer: B. Neutrality is the strategy, not just a feature. (Ch. 6)*
