## A note on private-company analysis

Public-company analysis leans on SEC filings: audited, standardized, quarterly. Pre-IPO analysis leans on:

- **Company-disclosed** facts (founder statements, company blog, hiring pages) — real, but self-selected.
- **Press-reported** figures (funding rounds, revenue run-rates) — often sourced from insiders, but unaudited and sometimes wrong.
- **Your hypotheses** — clearly labeled, never mixed with the two above.

Whenever this book gives a number, it tells you which of the three it is. That discipline is the whole game.
