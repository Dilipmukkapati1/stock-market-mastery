## The stock's 2026 story

AMD's share price ran from the mid-$200s to a **record intraday ~$649.88 (Oct 6, 2026)** — market cap crossing **$1 trillion** — up ~180–200% YTD at the peak (vs NVIDIA ~+21%), powered by the Meta/Anthropic deal flow, Citi's "CPU renaissance" thesis, and server-CPU TAM upgrades. Then reality checked: a ~9% drop after Q2 earnings (Aug 2026, flat margin guidance), and a **~19% October drawdown** (low $524.60 on Oct 20) on OpenAI-pace and AI-financing worries. At the Oct 22 close of **$507.93**, market cap ≈ **$829B**.

## Bull case

1. **The CPU franchise prints money while AI scales.** ~46% server revenue share and still gaining — this is a proven, profitable, share-stealing business funding the AI build-out.
2. **Only credible #2 in AI accelerators**, with inference parity demonstrated independently (MLPerf), HBM capacity advantages, and Helios rack systems shipping into committed demand.
3. **~14 GW of headline commitments** (OpenAI 6, Meta 6, Anthropic 2) de-risk the MI450 ramp — *if* they convert.
4. **Financial trajectory:** revenue +34% → guided +41%, gross margin expanding toward 56%+, FCF inflecting ($5.5B FY2025), net-cash balance sheet.
5. Management with a 12-year execution record and pay explicitly tied to the $600 milestone.

## Bear case

1. **The price assumes the story.** ~46x forward earnings and ~$829B for a company with $4.3B of GAAP net income: years of flawless execution are already paid for. The October drawdown showed how fast "priced for perfection" reprices.
2. **Commitments aren't backlog.** GW deals are conditional, milestone-based, and partly circular (penny warrants); conversion risk sits with customers whose own AI economics are unproven.
3. **19.6% contingent dilution** from the warrants lands precisely in the success scenario — per-share math lags headline math.
4. **NVIDIA + custom silicon** bracket AMD from both sides: CUDA's training moat above, hyperscaler ASICs below.
5. **Un-modelables:** China export policy ($800M of charges already), TSMC concentration, console cycle timing.

At ~$829B, the market is charging substantially for the option. Whether that's right depends on one operational question — does Helios ship on time into real gigawatts? — and Nov 3's earnings are the next hard data point.
