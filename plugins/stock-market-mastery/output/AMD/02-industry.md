# Part One · Section 2 — The Company

**The short version:** AMD is a fabless semiconductor company (Santa Clara, founded 1969, ~31,000 employees) that designs chips TSMC manufactures. It has quietly become, by revenue, primarily an **AI data-center company**.

**Three segments** (as AMD reports them):

| Segment | What it sells | FY2025 revenue | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Data Center** | EPYC server CPUs, Instinct AI GPUs, Pensando networking, FPGAs | $16.6B (+32%) | 48% |
| **Client** | Ryzen PC processors/APUs | $10.6B (+51%) | 31% |
| **Gaming** | Radeon GPUs + console chips (Sony/Microsoft) | $3.9B (+51%) | 11% |
| **Embedded** | Xilinx FPGAs, adaptive SoCs (industrial, auto, aerospace) | $3.5B (−3%) | 10% |

(Data Center + Client + Gaming are grouped in reporting as shown; totals = FY2025 revenue of $34.6B.)

By Q2 2026, Data Center was **58%** of quarterly revenue ($6.7B of $11.5B, +107% YoY) — the mix is shifting toward AI infrastructure every quarter.

**Built by acquisition.** Xilinx (2022, ~$49B — the embedded/FPGA business), ZT Systems (closed Mar 2025 — rack-scale design expertise behind Helios; manufacturing arm sold), and a **pending ~$8.2B all-stock acquisition of AI research lab World Labs** (announced Sep 28, 2026).

> **Source:** AMD Q4/FY2025 earnings release · 2026-02-03 · AMD 10-K FY2025 (employees, segments) · press reporting on World Labs deal · Sep 2026
