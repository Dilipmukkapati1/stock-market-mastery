# Part One · Section 1 — Management

**The short version:** AMD's management is the strongest part of the story. One CEO for twelve years, a stable bench, and a board literally paying the CEO to hit the AI roadmap.

**Dr. Lisa Su — Chair & CEO (since 2014).** Su joined AMD in 2012 from Freescale (where she was CTO), after a semiconductor R&D career at IBM. She is credited with one of the great turnarounds in tech history: AMD's market cap was ~$6B in 2016; it crossed $1 trillion in October 2026. Her FY2025 compensation was ~$55.2M, almost all equity (base salary $1.29M). She personally owns only ~0.2% of shares — her alignment runs through unvested equity and performance awards, not a founder-style stake.

**The bench.** Jean Hu (CFO since Jan 2023), Mark Papermaster (CTO — the Zen architecture era), Forrest Norrod (Data Center GM), Phil Guido (Chief Commercial Officer). No major C-suite departures surfaced in 2026.

**The signal in the 2026 pay packages.** Su received a $36M long-term equity award plus a special **"CEO Value Creation Equity Award" targeted at $75M**, whose top payout requires AMD's stock to reach **$600 before March 2031**. CFO and CTO awards ($9M / $10M) follow the same pattern. Boards write these when they want the AI strategy executed, not managed for safety.

**Governance notes.** Institutional ownership ~72.7% (BlackRock, Vanguard, State Street largest). Insider Form 4s in the last 180 days show sales only (mostly scheduled 10b5-1 plan sales — normal, but no insider open-market buying either).
