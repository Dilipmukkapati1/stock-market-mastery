| Gross margin | 49% | 50% | 54% |

| Diluted EPS | $1.00 | $2.65 | $1.38 |

Non-GAAP FY2025: gross margin 52%, operating income $7,768M, net income $6,831M, EPS $4.17. Q1 2026 revenue was $10,253M with record FCF of ~$2.57B.

> **Source:** AMD Q4/FY2025 earnings release · 2026-02-03 · AMD Q2 2026 earnings release · 2026-08-04

**The GAAP/non-GAAP gap — read it once, remember it forever.** AMD's GAAP operating income ($3.69B) is less than half its non-GAAP figure ($7.77B). The bridge: ~**$2.25B/year of amortization** from the Xilinx acquisition's intangibles (a real economic cost of buying growth, but non-cash and scheduled) plus **$1.64B of stock-based compensation** in FY2025 (a real cost to shareholders — dilution — that non-GAAP pretends away). Neither number is "the truth": GAAP overstates the drag (amortization of a 2022 deal says little about 2026 operations), non-GAAP understates it (employees must be paid, increasingly in stock). Sophisticated holders track both and watch SBC as % of revenue.

## Segment economics

| Segment | FY2024 | FY2025 | Growth | Q2 2026 | YoY |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Data Center | $12,579M | $16,635M | +32% | $6,718M | **+107%** |
| Client | $7,054M | $10,640M | +51% | $3,062M | +23% |
| Gaming | $2,595M | $3,910M | +51% | $779M | **−31%** |
| Embedded | $3,557M | $3,454M | −3% | $977M | +19% |

Three stories in one table. **Data Center is the company now**: Q2 2026's +107% reflects the Instinct ramp layering onto steady EPYC growth; management guides to data-center revenue roughly doubling again in 2027. **Client is a share-gain machine** (record 30.3% x86 unit share) compounding at 20–50%. **Gaming is the cyclical drag**: −31% in Q2 2026 as the console generation ages — semi-custom revenue fades until next-gen consoles (rumored 2027, unconfirmed). Embedded is the slow, steady ballast recovering from its inventory correction (+19%).

**The Instinct black box.** AMD does not break out Instinct revenue. Analyst estimates: ~$7–8B in 2025, $8–12B in 2026. That means most Data Center growth credibility rests on estimates and on the GW deal pipeline, not disclosed product revenue — a genuine disclosure gap investors should price.

## Balance sheet & cash

Cash + short-term investments: **$10,552M** (Dec 2025) → **$13.1B** (Jun 2026). Debt: $3,222M → ~$3.25B, then AMD issued **$4.75B of senior notes after Q2** (pro-forma gross debt ~$8B) — deliberately terming out funding ahead of the Helios/World Labs spending era while remaining **net cash**. Inventory at **$8.5B** is the line to watch: it's the physical pre-build for MI450/Helios — justified if the ramp lands, a write-down risk if it slips (AMD has form here: see the $800M MI308 China charge in the Risks chapter).

## Capital allocation

FY2025: operating cash flow $6,493M, capex just $974M (fabless = capital-light), FCF $5,519M. Buybacks: $1,316M in FY2025 (12.4M shares); ~$10B authorization with ~$9.2–9.4B remaining — but H1 2026 repurchases slowed to $221M and **Q2 2026 had zero open-market buys**. At $500+/share, management is signaling the stock is no longer the obvious bargain it was at $100 — or that capacity and deals (World Labs: ~$8.2B of stock) matter more. Both readings are defensible; neither is bearish about operations.

## Guidance credibility

Q3 2026 guide: **~$13.0B ± $300M** revenue (~+41% YoY) at ~56% non-GAAP gross margin. Note the stock fell ~9% after Q2 results (Aug 2026) despite the beat — the market punished *flat margin guidance*, a reminder that at this valuation, "good" is no longer good enough.
