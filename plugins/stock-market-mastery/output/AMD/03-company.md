# Part One · Section 3 — Financials (Snapshot)

**The short version:** A high-growth, increasingly profitable company with a fortress balance sheet — valued like it.

| | FY2024 | FY2025 | Q2 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $25.8B | **$34.6B** (+34%) | **$11.5B** (+50% YoY) |
| Gross margin (GAAP / non-GAAP) | 49% | 50% / 52% | 54% / 56% |
| Net income (GAAP) | $1.6B | $4.3B | $2.3B |
| Diluted EPS (GAAP / non-GAAP) | $1.00 | $2.65 / $4.17 | $1.38 / $1.66 |
| Free cash flow | $2.4B | **$5.5B** | $1.6B (Q1'26: record $2.57B) |

**Balance sheet.** Cash + short-term investments of **$13.1B** (Jun 2026) against ~$3.25B of debt at quarter-end — AMD is **net cash** even after issuing $4.75B of senior notes post-Q2 (pro-forma gross debt ~$8B). Inventory has built to $8.5B to support the AI ramp — worth watching (Part Two).

**Guidance.** Q3 2026 revenue ~**$13.0B ± $300M** (~+41% YoY), non-GAAP gross margin ~56%. Management has said data center revenue should **roughly double in 2027**.

**Capital returns.** ~$10B buyback authorization with ~$9.2–9.4B remaining — but repurchases slowed sharply in 2026 (zero open-market buys in Q2), suggesting management sees better uses for cash (capacity, the World Labs deal) or simply a rich stock price.

**One caution for readers of AMD's numbers:** GAAP and non-GAAP differ a lot — ~$2.25B/year of Xilinx acquisition amortization and $1.64B of stock comp sit in the gap. Part Two walks through what that means.

> **Source:** AMD Q4/FY2025 earnings release · 2026-02-03 · AMD Q2 2026 earnings release · 2026-08-04 (all figures company-disclosed)
