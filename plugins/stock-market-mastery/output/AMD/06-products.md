# Part One · Section 6 — Customers

**The short version:** AMD's growth is now contracted — via headline multi-gigawatt deals — to a handful of the largest AI buyers on earth. That's validation and concentration in the same sentence.

**The AI deals:**

- **OpenAI (Oct 2025):** up to **6 gigawatts** of Instinct GPUs across generations, starting with 1 GW of MI450 in 2H 2026 — plus a warrant for up to 160M AMD shares (Part Two explains the structure).
- **Meta (Feb 2026):** up to **6 GW** on a near-identical structure, 1 GW binding initially.
- **Anthropic (2026):** up to **2 GW** of MI450 in Helios racks from 1H 2027; AMD invests up to $5B in Anthropic.
- **Oracle:** OCI AI supercluster starting with **50,000 MI450 GPUs** from Q3 2026.
- **Microsoft:** Helios systems into Azure for inference. AMD's Helios deployment list also names HUMAIN, Cirrascale, TensorWave, Vultr; plus an AMD–Cisco–HUMAIN JV targeting 1 GW by 2030.

**The traditional base.** Every major cloud runs EPYC (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Oracle); OEMs (Dell, HPE, Lenovo) ship EPYC and Ryzen systems; Sony and Microsoft anchor semi-custom gaming.

**Read the deals carefully.** "Up to 6 GW" is a milestone-based commitment framework, **not booked backlog** — deployments scale if AMD delivers product and the customers' own AI economics hold. Part Two examines the warrant structure and the "circular deal" criticism in depth.

> **Source:** AMD/OpenAI announcement · Oct 2025 · press reporting on Meta/Anthropic/Oracle agreements · 2026 · AMD Helios disclosures · 2026
