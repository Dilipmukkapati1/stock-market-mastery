# Part One · Section 6 — Customers

NVIDIA now discloses its Data Center customers in two buckets — and in Q2 fiscal 2027 they were nearly equal (company figures):

- **Hyperscale: $48.7B (about 55% of Data Center).** The public clouds and largest consumer-internet companies — in practice Microsoft, Amazon, Google, Meta, Oracle (NVIDIA does not name them in the revenue table). They buy in entire data centers and sign for generations ahead.
- **ACIE — AI clouds, industrial and enterprise: $40.3B (about 45%).** The "neoclouds" (CoreWeave, Nebius, Lambda and peers), sovereign-AI programs (governments building national AI capacity), enterprises, and AI labs. This bucket's growth is the broadening story: in Jensen Huang's words, a year ago "one lab alone was driving the buildout"; now frontier labs, startups, sovereigns and industry buy in parallel.

**Concentration is the shadow.** For fiscal 2026, one direct customer was 22% of total revenue and another 14%. In Q2 fiscal 2027 one direct customer was ~16% of total revenue; across the first half of fiscal 2027, three direct customers were 16%, 15% and 13%. These are mostly intermediaries (system builders and clouds) rather than end users, but the arithmetic stands: a change of heart by two or three buyers moves the world's most valuable company.

**The customers are also the competitors.** Every hyperscaler runs a custom-silicon program — Google's TPU, Amazon's Trainium, Microsoft's Maia, Meta's MTIA — and buys AMD GPUs as a second source. They buy NVIDIA because they must (supply, software, time-to-market) while investing to need it less. Google committing up to a million TPUs to Anthropic from 2026 shows the second ecosystem reaching frontier scale.

**And increasingly, NVIDIA is its customers' financier.** A $30B investment in OpenAI (February 2026), guarantees capped at $105B behind OpenAI's Ohio data-center leases (August 2026), and ~$26B of commitments to rent back GPU capacity from cloud partners (as reported from its filings) mean NVIDIA now funds parts of the demand it books. Bulls call it ecosystem seeding; bears call it circular. Part Two, Deep Research 3, lays out both readings with the numbers.
