Three things to see in this table:

**The scale is without precedent.** Q2 fiscal 2027 revenue of $96.2B — $89.0B of it Data Center — is a quarterly revenue larger than the annual revenue of most Fortune 500 companies, growing at +106% year over year. Management guided Q3 fiscal 2027 to **$108B ±2%**.

**Profitability is software-like.** Fiscal 2026 net income was $120.1B on $215.9B of revenue (a ~56% net margin, helped by ~$8.9B of pre-tax gains on equity investments; operating income was ~60% of revenue). Free cash flow was ~$96.6B. Q2 fiscal 2027 operating income was $63.7B on $96.2B of revenue — a 66% operating margin. Read the net income line with care in fiscal 2027: investment gains are inflating it (Q1's $58.3B net on $53.5B of operating income is the tell), so Part Two tracks operating income and cash flow as the cleaner measures.

**The balance sheet changed in 2026.** NVIDIA returned ~$26.0B to shareholders in Q2 fiscal 2027 alone, and in September 2026 the board added $150B to the repurchase program (~$235B total authorization). But the same year it issued $25B of senior notes, invested $30B in OpenAI, and its operating cash flow halved in Q2 ($24.1B, from $50.3B in Q1) as it bought $15.8B of equity securities and took on a guarantee package capped at $105B behind a customer's data-center leases. The income statement still says "chip company"; the cash-flow statement now says "chip company plus financier of the AI buildout." Both are in Part Two.

**Watch next:** Q3 fiscal 2027 earnings, expected late November 2026 — the test is the $108B guide and whether the cash-flow picture normalizes.
