## TPU program (primary + labeled)
- Generation table: Google Cloud TPU documentation (v5p/v6e/TPU7x specs) and Google blogs; Ironwood announced Apr 9, 2025 (blog.google): first inference-designed TPU, 4,614 FP8 TFLOPS, 192GB HBM3e @ 7.37 TB/s, 9,216-chip pod = 42.5 exaflops, >4× Trillium per-chip, 2× perf/W; GA Mar 31, 2026 per Cloud TPU release notes (synthesis; Google blog Nov 6, 2025 guided "coming weeks").
- TPU 8t/8i (Google Cloud technical deep dive, Apr 22, 2026): 8t 12.6 FP4 PFLOPS, 216GB HBM, 9,600 chips = 121 exaflops, Virgo >134k chips, up to 2.7× perf/$ vs Ironwood (training); 8i 10.1 PFLOPS, 288GB HBM, 384MB SRAM, Boardfly ≤1,152 chips, +80% perf/$ (inference); both Axion hosts, ~2× perf/W; GA guided "later 2026" — Oct 2026 docs still listed TPU7x as latest available, no pricing disclosed.
- Design partners: Broadcom co-developer since inception (SerDes, ASIC implementation, TSMC coordination); Broadcom Form 8-K (Apr 6, 2026): develop/supply future TPUs + rack networking through 2031; Anthropic ~3.5GW via Broadcom from 2027. MediaTek cost-track role (incl. 8i "Zebrafish," 20–30% lower cost; Broadcom 8t "Sunfish") — trade-press reported, NOT Google-confirmed.
- Economics: SemiAnalysis (Nov 2025, via secondary): TPU TCO ~30% below GB200, ~44% internal. SemiAnalysis InferenceX (Sep 7, 2026, via secondary): Ironwood $0.181/1M tokens vs B200 $0.222 / B300 $0.276 (FP8, Qwen3.5-397B, 100 tok/s/user); TTFT worse (5.41s vs 3.75s/2.40s @256 users); no native FP4. Oct 2026 agentic benchmark: Google-submitted TPUv7 1.42–1.67× perf/$ vs GB300, with a detailed counter-reading (Pouladian) favoring GB300 by a wide margin on other configurations — DISPUTED, presented as such.
- Gemini 3 model card: trained on TPUs with JAX + ML Pathways. TorchTPU open-sourced Oct 20, 2026 (Google + Meta PyTorch team; ~94% of JAX throughput on one published test; 98% HF models ran; Meta production use reported).
