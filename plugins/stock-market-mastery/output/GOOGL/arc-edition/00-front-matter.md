**A note on timing.** This edition incorporates Alphabet's **Q3 2026 results** (quarter ended September 30, 2026), reported after the market close on **October 28, 2026** — the book was first finished that evening on Q2 figures and updated the same night at the reader's request. What Q3 showed, in brief: revenue of $127.58 billion (+25%), inside the $127.4–127.9 billion consensus range this book cited beforehand; Google Cloud accelerating to **+103%** ($30.71 billion), well past Q2's +82%, with TPU system sales a meaningful contributor; capex of $51.36 billion and a second straight negative free-cash-flow quarter (−$9.07 billion); the full-year 2026 capex guide held at $195–205 billion, with 2027 still guided only as "increase significantly" (detail promised on the Q4 call); and headline EPS of $2.12 depressed by an $8.66 billion loss on equity securities — stripped of that line, EPS was ~$2.67, below the $2.94–3.04 ex-gains consensus. The shares fell roughly 8–9% after hours. The full Q3 section is in Part One · 3 — Financials.
