# Part One · 6 — Customers

## Who pays Alphabet, in layers

**Advertisers** remain the foundation: ~73% of FY2025 revenue. Retail and financial services led Search growth in Q2 2026; YouTube draws the brand budgets. This layer is not this book's focus, but it is the funder of everything in it — Services generated $39.5 billion of operating income in a single quarter.

**Consumers** pay directly more than they used to: subscriptions, platforms and devices contributed $12.9 billion in Q2 2026 (+15%), across 325M+ paid subscriptions company-wide (end-2025), and the Gemini app's 950 million monthly users sit behind a freemium funnel into Google AI plans.

**Cloud customers** are where the silicon story lives. Google Cloud's roster now includes roughly 90% of the Fortune 100 on Gemini Enterprise, some 500 customers each processing over a trillion tokens a year, and — the marquee proof — **OpenAI**, which runs large-scale inference, including for ChatGPT, on Google Cloud's NVIDIA instances (A4X/A4X Max). A rival's flagship product serving from your data centers is the strongest customer reference in the industry.

**Anthropic — the customer that changed the business.** Anthropic is to the TPU what a launch customer is to an airliner. The sequence: years of TPU use; **October 23, 2025** — an expansion to *up to one million TPUs*, tens of billions of dollars, over a gigawatt of capacity in 2026; **April 6, 2026** — a further agreement with Google *and Broadcom* for **multiple gigawatts of next-generation TPU capacity from 2027** (Broadcom's filing quantified roughly 3.5 GW), Anthropic's largest compute commitment to date, announced alongside run-rate revenue above $30 billion; **April 24, 2026** — Google committed to invest up to **$40 billion** in Anthropic ($10 billion immediately at a $350 billion valuation, the rest contingent), alongside at least 5 GW of compute over five years, as reported by Reuters. SemiAnalysis projects Anthropic will surpass DeepMind itself as the largest TPU user by 2029. Note the interlock: Alphabet is simultaneously Anthropic's landlord, chip supplier, and a major shareholder — and Anthropic's valuation gains are a material part of the $99 billion gain in Alphabet's Q2 income statement. The customer, the supplier, and the investor are the same three parties, in a triangle this book returns to in Part Two.

**The reported and the new.** **Meta** has been reported (The Information, November 2025 and February 2026) to be in talks to spend billions on TPUs for its own data centers from 2027 and to have signed a multibillion-dollar TPU rental deal — *neither company has confirmed this*, and the book treats it as reported, not fact. **Oracle** is confirmed and new (October 27, 2026): TPUs on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure from 2027 — a hyperscaler reselling Google's silicon. **Fluidstack and the Blackstone venture (Crux AI)** are channel customers: they operate TPU capacity that Google finances or backstops. Older TPU tenants named in Google materials — Apple (Apple Intelligence training), Salesforce, Midjourney, Safe Superintelligence — show the rental base predates the current wave.

**Waymo's customers** are simply riders: 500,000+ paid rides a week and climbing, in a market where Alphabet is effectively the only scaled U.S. operator.

The customer picture in one sentence: Alphabet has the broadest demand base of any AI company — advertisers, consumers, enterprises, its biggest model rival, a frontier lab it part-owns, and now other clouds — and that breadth, more than any single benchmark, is what the $195–205 billion capex guide is being spent to serve.
