# Part One · 4 — The Product

## What Alphabet actually sells

**Search and YouTube (the annuity).** Search is still the most valuable product in technology: $63.3 billion of quarterly revenue, and in 2026 it is being rebuilt around AI — **AI Overviews reach 2.5 billion monthly users** and **AI Mode passed one billion monthly users** in its first year. The strategic question of the decade — does AI answer engines kill the golden goose? — is so far being answered with growth: Search revenue +17% in Q2 2026, with management crediting AI features for incremental query growth. YouTube adds $11.1 billion of quarterly ads plus a large slice of the $12.9 billion subscriptions line; YouTube's ads-plus-subscriptions revenue passed $60 billion in 2025.

**Gemini (the challenger).** The Gemini family is Alphabet's flagship model line: **Gemini 3** (November 18, 2025) launched at the top of LMArena (1,501 Elo) with benchmark-leading reasoning (GPQA Diamond 91.9% for Pro, 93.8% for Deep Think). The 2026 cadence has been rapid — 3.5 Flash at I/O (May 19, 2026), 3.6 Flash and 3.5 Flash-Lite (July 21, 2026), 3.5 Pro in testing — and **Gemini 4's pre-training run, which Pichai called Alphabet's most ambitious yet, began in mid-2026**. The Gemini app reached **950 million monthly users** in Q2 2026; across all products Google processes **3.2 quadrillion tokens a month** (May 2026, seven times the prior year), and its APIs run at roughly 22 billion tokens a minute. Crucially for this book: the Gemini model card states Gemini 3 was **trained on Google's own TPUs** with JAX and ML Pathways — the flagship product of the AI era runs on Alphabet's own silicon end to end.

**Google Cloud (the platform).** Cloud sells AI infrastructure as a menu: its own TPU instances (v5e/v5p, Trillium, Ironwood; 8t/8i announced), NVIDIA instances (A3 H100/H200, A4 B200, A4X GB200 NVL72, A4X Max GB300, and A5X Vera Rubin planned for November 2026 general availability), the Vertex AI / Gemini Enterprise agent platform, and the data and security stack (including Wiz). Nearly **90% of the Fortune 100 use Gemini Enterprise**, per the Q2 call.

**The TPU as a product (the new line).** 2026 added a fourth product category that did not exist as a business before: **silicon itself**. Google now (a) rents TPUs on its cloud, (b) **sells TPU systems into customers' own data centers** — revenue recognized for the first time in Q2 2026, with the bulk expected in 2027, (c) supplies TPU capacity to third-party operators it finances or backstops (Fluidstack/TeraWulf, the Blackstone joint venture Crux AI), and (d) from 2027, will offer TPUs **on another hyperscaler's cloud** — Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, announced October 27, 2026. A component became a catalog.

**Waymo (the physical product).** Over 500,000 paid driverless rides a week (April 2026), a fleet above 4,000 vehicles including the new Ojai sixth-generation vehicle, roughly 14–15 U.S. metros after the September 2026 openings in Denver, San Diego, Tampa and Las Vegas, and a target of one million rides a week by end-2026. It is the most mature robotaxi operation in the world — and still inside "Other Bets."
