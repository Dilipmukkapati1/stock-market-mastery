# Part One · 5 — Product Quality

## Is it good? The evidence, product by product

**Models.** Gemini 3's launch benchmarks were frontier-class across the board — LMArena 1,501 Elo (first above 1,500), GPQA Diamond 91.9%/93.8%, Humanity's Last Exam 37.5%/41.0% with Deep Think, SWE-bench Verified 76.2% — and, more telling than any benchmark, the usage: 950 million monthly app users, AI Mode past a billion users in year one, and 3.2 quadrillion tokens a month across products. The 2026 follow-through (3.5/3.6 Flash generations tuned for speed and cost, Gemini 4 in pre-training) shows a lab iterating on a schedule, not defending a single launch. The caveat is organizational: the August 2026 DeepMind reshuffle put product leadership over the lab and cost it two of its most storied researchers — quality over the next two model generations is the thing it puts at risk.

**Silicon.** Ironwood (TPU v7, generally available March 31, 2026 per Google Cloud release notes) is a serious inference machine on paper: 4,614 FP8 TFLOPS per chip, 192 GB of HBM3e at 7.37 TB/s, and 9,216-chip pods delivering 42.5 exaflops — Google claims more than 4× Trillium's per-chip performance and 2× its performance per watt. The honest quality assessment has two asterisks, both developed in Part Two: independent cost-per-token results are genuinely contested (SemiAnalysis's own September–October 2026 work can be read both ways), and the announced successors (TPU 8t/8i) were still listed as "coming soon" in Google Cloud documentation in late October 2026 — the shipping flagship is Ironwood, not the 8-series.

**The software gap is closing.** Historically the TPU's weakness was never the chip; it was that the world's AI code is written for CUDA. Google's answer arrived October 20, 2026: **TorchTPU**, a native PyTorch backend built with Meta's PyTorch team and open-sourced, replacing the awkward PyTorch/XLA path. Reported results: 98% of popular Hugging Face models ran without modification, and PyTorch training reached ~94% of JAX's throughput in a published test; Meta reported SGLang on TorchTPU running in production. It is early, but this is the single most important quality improvement for the TPU's *external* business — it attacks the exact friction that kept TPUs a Google-only tool.

**Cloud execution.** An 82% revenue growth quarter at a 35.6% operating margin, a $514 billion backlog, record market share (15% of the global cloud infrastructure market in Q2 2026, per Synergy Research, behind AWS's 28% and Azure's 20%) — the quality verdict on Cloud operations is no longer arguable. The disclosed wrinkle: management warned that bridging with **third-party capacity** in Q3 2026 would modestly pressure Cloud margins while internal capacity catches up — a quality-of-supply problem created by demand, not by product. In the event (Q3 reported October 28, 2026), Cloud revenue growth accelerated to +103% and the margin *expanded* to 36.2%, so the feared pressure did not show up in the print — though management repeated the warning for the quarters ahead as third-party usage ramps further.

**Search monetization quality.** The fear was that AI answers would destroy click economics. Two years in, the data says otherwise so far: Search revenue +17% again, paid clicks +13% in Q2 2026, with management describing AI Overviews queries as monetizing encouragingly. Management itself flagged tougher year-ago comparisons from Q3 2026 — and they arrived on schedule: in Q3, Search growth slowed to +12%, even as management reported the fastest year-over-year query growth in five years. The monetization question is now about conversion of that query growth, not about usage.
