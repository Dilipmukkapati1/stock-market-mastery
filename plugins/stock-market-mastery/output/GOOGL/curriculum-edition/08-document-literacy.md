# Document Literacy — Google (Alphabet) (GOOGL)

> Skills chapter · not investment advice · generated 2026-10-31

This chapter teaches you to read Alphabet's primary documents directly. Every exercise uses the *actual* current filings. Rule of the chapter: **a raw exercise must be completed before you claim the level.**

## 1 · The earnings press release (Exhibit 99.1 to Form 8-K)

**What it is for:** The fastest official summary of the quarter — segment revenue and operating income, the consolidated statements, and the footnotes where the one-time items hide. Latest: Q3 2026 release, furnished **2026-10-28** (Alphabet investor relations and SEC EDGAR, CIK 0001652044, 8-K exhibits).

**Learner levels:** L0 — find headline revenue/EPS. L1 — find segment margins. L2 — find the non-GAAP or footnote adjustments. L3 — reconcile the release to the 10-Q. L4 — brief the quarter from the release alone, with every number sourced.

**Raw exercise:** In the Q3 2026 release, find the footnote quantifying the equity-securities loss (−$8,664M; −$0.55 EPS). Highlight the exact sentence. Then find "Total revenues" and "Total costs and expenses" and verify operating income by subtraction ($127,577M − $87,001M = $40,576M — check the printed totals as you do it).

**Self-check:**
1. Where does TAC appear — its own line, or inside cost of revenues detail? (Its own disclosed line in segment/cost detail.)
2. Is the release's EPS GAAP or adjusted? (GAAP — the adjustment work is yours, Layer 4.)
3. Which segment table line tells you Cloud's margin without a calculator? (None — compute it: $11,121M ÷ $30,709M.)

## 2 · The 10-Q / 10-K (quarterly and annual reports)

**What it is for:** The audited-grade detail: full statements, footnotes (commitments, contingencies, segment note), MD&A, and risk factors. Latest: **10-Q for Q3 2026, filed 2026-10-29**; **10-K for FY2025, filed 2026-02-05** (SEC EDGAR, CIK 0001652044).

**Learner levels:** L0 — locate the three statements. L1 — find one footnote number used in this book. L2 — read MD&A's explanation of a margin move. L3 — compare risk factors across two filings for what changed. L4 — build a quarter's story from the 10-Q alone.

**Raw exercise:** In the Q3 2026 10-Q, open the commitments note and find the three figures from Layer 7 ($924.4B purchase commitments; $130.5B uncommenced leases; ~$98.5B contingent arrangements). Write down the note's own category labels — they differ slightly from this book's paraphrase, and learning the filer's vocabulary is the point.

**Self-check:**
1. Which document would you open for server useful-life policy? (The 10-K's significant-accounting-policies note.)
2. The Wiz acquisition closed March 11, 2026 for $29.5B — in which 2026 filing would you first find it completed? (Q1 2026 10-Q.)
3. True or false: risk factors in a 10-Q are usually incorporated by reference to the 10-K unless updated. (True — check for updates.)

## 3 · The proxy statement (DEF 14A)

**What it is for:** Governance truth: who controls the votes, what executives are paid, related-party transactions, and shareholder proposals. Latest: **DEF 14A filed 2026-04-24**, record date 2026-04-06.

**Learner levels:** L0 — find the meeting date. L1 — find the beneficial-ownership table. L2 — compute founder voting control. L3 — read the compensation discussion for what behavior pay actually rewards. L4 — assess whether any proposal could pass against founder opposition (Layer 3's lesson, applied).

**Raw exercise:** From the ownership table, verify Page 27.4% + Brin 25.3% = 52.7% of voting power, and identify which share class produces it (Class B, ten votes per share).

**Self-check:**
1. Do Class A and Class C differ in economics or in votes? (Votes.)
2. Where in the proxy would a related-party transaction with a founder entity appear? (The related-person transactions section.)
3. Why does the proxy matter more at Alphabet than at a single-class company? (Because control is locked — the document tells you whose preferences govern.)

## 4 · The earnings call transcript

**What it is for:** Management's emphasis, in their words — prepared remarks vs the Q&A, where analysts push on the gaps. Latest: Q3 2026 call, **2026-10-28** (transcripts via the usual providers; Alphabet posts the release, not the transcript — note the provenance difference).

**Learner levels:** L0 — separate prepared remarks from Q&A. L1 — list every forward-looking statement. L2 — map each claim to a number in the release. L3 — identify what analysts asked that management declined to answer. L4 — write the "emphasis delta": what management talked about most vs what the numbers say mattered most.

**Raw exercise:** Take Ashkenazi's TPU sequence (backlog → inventory → revenue) and Kurian's "~100 TPU customers." For each: which release line or 10-Q note would confirm it next quarter, and what would disconfirming evidence look like?

**Self-check:**
1. Which Q3 statements were guidance? (2026 capex held at $195–205B; 2027 detail deferred to the Q4 call.)
2. What did management flag as a Q4 margin pressure? (Third-party capacity.)
3. True or false: a transcript is a primary SEC document. (False — it is a company/ vendor record of a live event; the 8-K release is the filed document.)
