# Chapter 1 — Mercor at a Glance

Mercor is an AI talent marketplace turned AI-training-data company. It vets domain experts — engineers, doctors, lawyers, PhDs — with AI-run interviews, then sells their time to frontier AI labs that need human expertise to train and evaluate models. In under three years it went from dorm-room recruiting to a reported $2B gross revenue run rate and a $10B valuation.

## Why Mercor matters right now

Three live storylines define the company in October 2026:

1. **The $20B question.** In July 2026 Bloomberg reported Mercor in talks to raise $500M at a $20B valuation, with Nvidia reportedly discussing an investment. As of October 1, no close has been reported — the round is a rumor with good sourcing, not a price.
2. **The concentration problem.** Frontier labs drive nearly all revenue — and Meta paused *all* Mercor contracts in April 2026 after a data breach. One customer's decision moved the whole company.
3. **The credibility gap.** Reported growth is historic ($1M → $2B gross in ~3 years), but the headline numbers have drawn public scrutiny from analysts, and every figure in this book carries the label it deserves.

The rest of this book works through each of those in order: the product, the story, the numbers, the customers, the competition, and the risks.
