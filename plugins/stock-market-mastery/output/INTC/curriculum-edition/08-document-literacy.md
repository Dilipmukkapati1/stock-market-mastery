# Document Literacy — Intel (INTC)

> Skills chapter · not investment advice · generated 2026-10-31

This chapter teaches you to read Intel's primary documents directly. Every exercise uses the *actual* current filings. Rule of the chapter: **a raw exercise must be completed before you claim the level.**

## 1 · The earnings press release

**What it is for:** The quarter in official summary — segment tables, GAAP-to-non-GAAP reconciliations, and the guidance table that moves the stock. Latest: Q3 2026 release, **2026-10-29** (Intel investor relations; also furnished as an 8-K exhibit).

**Learner levels:** L0 — find headline revenue/EPS. L1 — find segment operating income. L2 — read the non-GAAP reconciliation line by line. L3 — tie the guidance table to the call's language. L4 — brief the quarter from the release alone.

**Raw exercise:** In the Q3 2026 release, find (a) the Foundry segment's operating loss (−$1,853M) and (b) the Q4 guidance table (revenue $15–16B; GM 37.1%/38.0%; EPS $0.10/$0.25). Then find the disclosure quantifying the Fab 28 incident's expected impact (~$300M, ~200bp). Note which of these three items the headlines you saw actually mentioned.

**Self-check:**
1. Where does "external revenue" for Foundry appear in the release — or does it? (The detailed external figure, $411M, is in the 10-Q's segment note — a lesson in release-vs-filing depth.)
2. Is the headline EPS GAAP or non-GAAP? (The release leads with both; know which one a headline used: GAAP $0.60, non-GAAP $0.52.)
3. Which single table will matter most next quarter? (Guidance — Layer 5's lesson.)

## 2 · The 10-Q / 10-K

**What it is for:** The audited-grade layer: segment notes with external/intersegment splits, commitments, the escrow instrument's accounting, and risk factors. Latest: **10-Q for Q3 2026, filed 2026-10-30, accession 0000050863-26-000191**; **10-K for FY2025** (SEC EDGAR, CIK 0000050863).

**Learner levels:** L0 — locate the statements. L1 — find one number used in this book in its note. L2 — read MD&A on foundry losses. L3 — diff the risk factors across two filings. L4 — reconstruct the turnaround's financial state from the 10-Q alone.

**Raw exercise:** Three finds: (1) the segment note's Foundry **external revenue of $411M** for Q3 and the disclosure that it arose mostly from one non-recurring contract; (2) the **$19.6B capex commitments** figure; (3) the accounting discussion of the **government escrow shares** behind the nine months' $12.614B mark-to-market losses. Write down each note's title as Intel words it — filer vocabulary is the skill.

**Self-check:**
1. Why does the segment note matter more than the release for foundry analysis? (External vs intersegment lives there.)
2. Where would the 14A gating language appear — MD&A, risk factors, or both? (Both, in substance; find each.)
3. True or false: the escrow MTM losses involved cash leaving Intel. (False — non-cash fair-value changes.)

## 3 · The deal documents (Forms 8-K, 2025)

**What it is for:** The primary record of the rescue-era transactions: the U.S. government investment (August 2025), SoftBank (September 2025), NVIDIA (September 2025 announcement; closed December 2025), and the Altera sale (closed September 12, 2025). When a summary anywhere describes these deals, these filings are the court of appeal.

**Learner levels:** L0 — find the share counts and prices. L1 — find the escrow mechanics. L2 — find the warrant's trigger (<51% foundry ownership). L3 — identify what each agreement does *not* contain (board seats, purchase commitments). L4 — draft the one-paragraph deal summary a professional would sign.

**Raw exercise:** From the government-investment 8-K materials, extract: shares (433.3M), price ($20.47), funding sources (CHIPS $5.695B + Secure Enclave $3.175B), escrow portion (~159M shares), warrant (240.5M shares at $20 on the foundry-ownership trigger). Then state, in one sentence, what the U.S. government did *not* get.

**Self-check:**
1. Did NVIDIA's agreement commit it to buy Intel products? (No purchase commitment was disclosed — the partnership is co-development.)
2. What did SoftBank's $2B buy beyond shares? (Nothing contractual that was disclosed — no board seat, no purchase commitment.)
3. Altera: what percentage was sold, to whom, at what valuation? (51%, Silver Lake, $8.75B.)

## 4 · The earnings call transcript

**What it is for:** Management's emphasis and the analysts' pressure points. Latest: Q3 2026 call, **2026-10-29** (vendor transcripts — provenance matters; the filed document is the release).

**Learner levels:** L0 — separate prepared remarks from Q&A. L1 — list forward-looking statements. L2 — map claims to release numbers. L3 — catalogue what was asked and not answered (yields, customer names, dates). L4 — write the emphasis delta vs the numbers.

**Raw exercise:** Take three call statements — Zinsner's PC slowdown "at least through H1 2027," the description of Xeon demand as tight, and Tan's 14A customer gating — and for each, name the future filing line that will confirm or refute it (client revenue; DCAI revenue and margins; foundry external revenue and any 8-K customer announcement).

**Self-check:**
1. Which number in the release does "tight" Xeon demand correspond to? (DCAI $7.318B, +78%.)
2. Did management quantify 18A yields on the call? (No — directional color only; that non-answer is data.)
3. True or false: a transcript is an SEC-filed document. (False.)
